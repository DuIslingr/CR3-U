Optional original card artwork for Chaos Rings III

Options > Graphics > Original Card Art enables the original pictures for cards 80, 81 and 86.
The matching names and N/R/SR profiles switch too: Little Red Riding Hood, Aphrodite, Adam.
English/fan-English uses translated original profiles; Japanese keeps the original text.
Android already contains the originals for 80 and 81. To supply the missing original for 86,
copy BOTH files from your own decrypted copy here:

Vita: CRDBcard01.psp2.mvgl and CRDBcardhd.psp2.mvgl
or iOS: CRDBcard01.ios.mvgl and CRDBcardhd.ios.mvgl

Subfolders work. No main archive is required. These files supply artwork only.
Turn the setting Off and On after copying while the game is running.
With iOS or Vita as your gameplay data, all three originals are already available.
The port does not include game artwork.
